Accordion

Pull a sample from the blowdown line once it runs cool, then test for pH, conductivity, and sulfite. Each takes under a minute with the drop-count kit — we walk you through all three in the included guide.

Dosage depends on your feedwater volume and test readings — start with the baseline on the label, test after 24 hours, and adjust. Your test kit tells you exactly where you stand.

Chemical-splash goggles and nitrile gloves cover most handling. Anything that needs more than that is flagged on the product page before you buy.
